# Ariane presentation media

Captured for the 0.2.0-alpha.1 presentation on 2 November 2026.

## Provenance

The product imagery comes from **Ariane running natively on macOS**. The sidebar is closed. The film edits actual window recordings: typing, opening a source, editing Markdown, nesting and moving outline branches, changing focus, searching, changing counters, starting Pomodoro and comparing drafts. Crops, speed changes, camera moves, brief feature labels and the closing wordmark are editorial. Product controls, results and interactions were not generated or recreated.

The 48-second film uses the separate **The Human Thread — Motion** project, with three pages and four fictional research notes. All people, institutions, interviews and observations are invented. The [portable essay and research material](../../examples/the-human-thread) remains available separately. Native demo projects, source recordings, editable production files and audio masters are kept in the local Ariane Showcase folder.

The dictation shot shows the native active dictation controls. A separate test sent an OpenAI-generated spoken phrase through Ariane's bundled local transcription engine, which returned the expected words. Speaker playback into the microphone did not produce a complete UI transcription; the film does not represent that test as a successful end-to-end microphone demonstration. Ariane's dictation remains local; the film's voice-over was generated separately.

## Sound and accessibility

The English narration is **AI-generated speech**, using the **Brian** voice from [Eleven v4 on Fal](https://fal.ai/models/elevenlabs/tts/eleven-v4), exported as uncompressed 48 kHz PCM. The performance is edited at phrase boundaries to follow the visible actions. The original instrumental score and editorial foley use synthesized tones and noise, without third-party music or recorded sound samples. Foley is part of the film's sound design, not a recording of application sounds.

`ariane-film.mp4` is the **1920 × 1080, 60 fps** H.264 film with a 48 kHz stereo AAC mix and an optional English subtitle track. The mix was normalized to approximately −16 LUFS with a −1.5 dBTP ceiling before AAC encoding. Standalone captions: [WebVTT](ariane-film.en.vtt) · [SRT](ariane-film.en.srt).

`film-poster.jpg` is the closing wordmark frame. The README leads with the native writing page and does not autoplay animation. The light wordmark preserves the original logo's alpha silhouette for contrast in GitHub's dark theme. GitHub releases remain withdrawn pending a notarized distribution.

The still images are close crops of the native app. `outliner.jpg` and `focus.jpg` are frames from the film, including their small editorial feature labels. `versions.jpg` places two exact excerpts from the native side-by-side comparison above one another for readability.
