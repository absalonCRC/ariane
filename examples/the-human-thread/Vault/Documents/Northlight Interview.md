# Northlight Interview

## A seam worth keeping

Mara: “That is where I changed my mind. If you erase the seam, the reader loses the journey.”

Jonas: “Then the revision should make the change of mind legible, not make it disappear.”

## Context

This invented dialogue supports the chapter A second voice. The speakers are [[Mara Vale]] and [[Jonas Reed]] at the fictional [[Northlight Writing Lab]].
