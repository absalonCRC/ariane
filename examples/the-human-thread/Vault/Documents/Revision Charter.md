# Revision Charter

## Five working principles

1. Keep a version before changing direction.
2. Open the source before relying on it.
3. Distinguish observation from interpretation.
4. Accept a suggestion only after reading it in context.
5. Leave the last word to the author.

This charter belongs to the fictional [[Northlight Writing Lab]]. It is a demonstration of source notes and editorial research, not a policy imposed by Ariane.
