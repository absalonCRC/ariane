# Mara Vale

Mara Vale is the invented author of The Human Thread. She works with editor [[Jonas Reed]] at the fictional [[Northlight Writing Lab]].

## Working practice

Mara keeps research beside her manuscript, records alternative openings and revises by asking what each sentence contributes. Her central idea is the [[Human Thread]]: the continuity of intention across a piece of writing.
