# Jonas Reed

Jonas Reed is an invented editor and Mara Vale’s conversation partner at [[Northlight Writing Lab]].

He asks three questions of every draft: what changed, why it changed, and what evidence remains visible. The fictional [[Northlight Interview]] records his disagreement with Mara about fluency and voice.

This profile and all attributed dialogue are demonstration material.
