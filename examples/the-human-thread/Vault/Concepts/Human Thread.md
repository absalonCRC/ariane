# Human Thread

The Human Thread is the central metaphor of this fictional essay. It names the line of intention that survives research, revision and changes of direction.

## Keep the author close

A writing tool can retrieve a note or preserve an earlier draft. The author decides what to claim, what to leave uncertain and what to remove.

The metaphor connects [[Mara Vale]]’s opening question with the [[Revision Charter]]. It is a concept invented for the demo, not an established academic theory.
