# Editorial Friction

Editorial friction is a working term in the fictional Northlight essay. It describes a useful interruption: a conflicting source, an awkward transition or a question that prevents a polished sentence from becoming an empty one.

[[Jonas Reed]] treats friction as an invitation to look again. [[Mara Vale]] treats it as a record of changing her mind.

This is demonstration prose, not a validated research finding.
