# Northlight Writing Lab

Northlight Writing Lab is a fictional workshop used in this demonstration. It has no real participants, institutional affiliation or published findings.

## The experiment

[[Mara Vale]] and [[Jonas Reed]] compare three ways of working: an unaided draft, a draft surrounded by research notes, and a draft revised through explicit editorial questions.

Their observations are written as an essay, not a scientific study. See [[Revision Charter]] and [[Source Ledger]].
